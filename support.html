Math Blitz Support

Questions, a bug, a name to report, or a data request? Email clyonkj@gmail.com. We reply within a few days.

Tell us your player name and what phone or iPad you use. For a bug, say what you tapped and what happened.

Common questions

  • How do I play with friends? Tap + on the rooms list and pick Locked room. Share the 4-digit key. Friends tap the room and type the key.
  • Who are the practice players? When open rooms are quiet, the app fills empty seats with computer players so you're not left waiting. They never join locked or crew rooms.
  • How do I report or block someone? In a race, long-press their name, or tap “…” next to them on the results.
  • How do I stop the 7 pm reminder? Me tab, then turn off the evening reminder.
  • How do I delete my data? Me tab, then Delete account (signed in with Apple) or Delete my data. Parents can also email us.
